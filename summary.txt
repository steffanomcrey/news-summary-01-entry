World news — Wednesday 11 November 2026
1. Ukraine and Russia held second-day US-mediated peace talks in Abu Dhabi, their first in nine months; Marco Rubio called them "very productive", but no breakthrough came (AP).
2. Iran said a tanker caught fire on hitting a naval mine in the Strait of Hormuz; UKMTO reported a tanker incident the same morning, unverified by Reuters (Reuters).
3. COP31 opened in Antalya, Turkey, with UN chief António Guterres calling it "make or bake time" and proposing a climate justice compact; the US was absent for a second year (AFP).
4. Democrat Scott Colom beat incumbent Republican Cindy Hyde-Smith in Mississippi's Senate race, 49.2% to 48.7% — Mississippi's first Democratic senator elected since 1982 (AP).
5. Congo's Ebola outbreak — the Bundibugyo species, with no vaccine or treatment — reached 11,042 confirmed cases and 5,387 deaths, its deadliest ever (Reuters).
6. US prosecutors charged three Iran-backed operatives over 18 alleged attacks in Europe and the US, including surveillance of RAF Fairford; Iran's London embassy rejected the allegations (BBC).
7. Millions of Spanish workers held the country's first general strike in 14 years, over a housing crisis, weeks before a snap election (Reuters).
8. Asian shares slipped as US October inflation came in hot — CPI up 0.4%, core up 0.3%, both above forecasts — hardening bets on a December Fed rate rise (NDTV Profit).
9. The US House voted 211–204 to shelve 8 impeachment articles against Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth by sending them to committee (BBC).
10. President Bukele said Salvadoran and Haitian forces destroyed the HQ of Haiti's 400 Mawozo gang; a reported toll of about 20 dead was unverified (BBC).
11. A Florida judge let Donald Trump's $10 billion defamation suit against the BBC, over a misleadingly edited speech clip, proceed (Reuters).
12. A bus rammed 32 vehicles in Seongnam, South Korea, injuring 57 people, nine seriously; the driver blamed brake failure (Yonhap).
